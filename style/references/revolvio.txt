Otamiser Revenue Management vs Revolvio
Both services run dynamic pricing and listing optimization for short-term rentals. Revolvio is a family-owned STR marketing and pricing service working with everything from single-unit owners to property management companies. Otamiser runs OTA ranking, pricing, and listing content across your full channel mix as one connected, daily operation. This page covers what each service does, where they differ, and which one fits your portfolio.
‍
What Revolvio Does
Revolvio is a family-owned short-term rental marketing and pricing agency serving clients worldwide, with no minimum portfolio size, from single-unit owners to property management companies.
Its core services are Listing Optimization (keyword and content updates across Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and Expedia) and Dynamic Pricing (rate management directed through a PriceLabs subscription, with a dedicated revenue expert layered on top), sold separately or bundled. Revolvio also offers SEO and PPC campaigns for direct bookings.
What Otamiser Does
Otamiser is a managed vacation rental revenue management service built for property managers and property management companies managing over 15 listings across multiple channels. The service connects three layers: rank, pricing, and channel mix, managed daily as one operation across Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, and Expedia.
OTA ranking is monitored daily across every platform in your mix. Rank moves before bookings do; when a listing starts losing visibility on any channel, Otamiser diagnoses which factors are driving it and acts before it costs bookings. Pricing runs as a full strategy on top of that signal, dynamic pricing, seasonal pricing, gap-night pricing, minimum-stay rules, and comp-set analysis, all calibrated to your market, booking pace, and ranking position, using tools like PriceLabs and Wheelhouse directed by our team. Listing content is updated monthly in line with each OTA's algorithm, with channel mix and rate parity managed across every platform.
Otamiser manages 25,000+ listings globally and has generated $2M+ in incremental revenue for clients in the last 12 months. The minimum is 15 listings, built for operators running a revenue function, not optimizing a single property.
Where the Two Differ
OTA ranking tracked as its own daily signal
Both services optimize listing content to adapt to each platform's algorithm. Otamiser goes a step further and monitors OTA ranking itself daily as a distinct metric, diagnosing the specific factors behind any drop and calibrating pricing to where a listing sits in the rankings that day.
One connected operation, not separate paid add-ons
Revolvio sells Listing Optimization, Dynamic Pricing, Listing Audits, and SEO/PPC campaigns as distinct products, each billed on its own, with per-channel add-on fees for listing optimization. Otamiser runs ranking, pricing, and listing content as one continuous, done-for-you operation under a single engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions
We're already talking to Revolvio. Why consider Otamiser?
Revolvio covers listing optimization and dynamic pricing well, especially for single units and smaller portfolios that want a dedicated expert without a portfolio minimum. Otamiser goes further: OTA ranking is tracked daily as its own metric across every major channel, and ranking, pricing, and listing content run as one connected system rather than separate paid services.
Does Otamiser replace my pricing tool?
No. Otamiser directs tools like PriceLabs and Wheelhouse on your behalf, so nothing needs to be replaced or migrated.
Both services optimize listing content. What's different?
Revolvio optimizes titles, descriptions, and keywords per platform, billed per additional channel. Otamiser runs the same layer on a fixed cadence, titles and descriptions updated monthly across your full channel mix, under one engagement rather than a per-channel add-on.
What's the minimum portfolio size for Otamiser?
15 listings for STR portfolios. Independent hotels are served individually. For operators below Otamiser's 15-listing minimum, Revolvio may be the better fit.
How fast will I see results with Otamiser?
70% of clients see ranking improvements within 30 days. Revenue impact typically shows within the first full month, tracked in your regular performance report.
