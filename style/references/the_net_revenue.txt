Otamiser Revenue Management vs The Net Revenue
Both services run pricing and distribution strategy for hotels, backed by a dedicated revenue team. The Net Revenue is a consultancy built for boutique hotels and hotel chains. Otamiser is a hotel revenue management company that runs OTA ranking, pricing, and channel content across Booking.com, Expedia, and Agoda as one connected daily operation. This page covers what each service does, where they differ, and which one fits your property.
What The Net Revenue Does
The Net Revenue is a revenue management consultancy for boutique hotels and hotel chains. Its core service, Revenue Management Outsourcing, acts as an extension of a hotel's team: demand forecasting, commercial strategy, distribution optimization, and weekly and monthly reporting. The Net Revenue also offers a standalone audit, consulting, hotel marketing services, and commercial representation, directing tools like SiteMinder, Cloudbeds, and RoomPriceGenie.
What Otamiser Does
Otamiser is a hotel revenue management company, running pricing and OTA ranking as one connected operation for independent hotels and small groups, from a handful of rooms to around 80.
OTA ranking is monitored daily across Booking.com, Expedia, and Agoda: Genius and Preferred Partner status, Expedia placement, and Agoda visibility are actively managed rather than switched on and left alone. Rank moves before bookings do, so when a property starts losing visibility, Otamiser diagnoses the cause and acts before it costs bookings. Pricing runs on top of that signal: rates, minimum-stay rules, and rate plans, calibrated daily to demand, seasonality, and comp set, on top of the RMS a hotel already runs. Listing content, titles, descriptions, photos, and room types, is tuned to how each channel ranks and converts, with channel mix and rate parity managed so no channel undercuts another.
Otamiser reports monthly on visibility, ADR, RevPAR, and revenue, with a three-month minimum to start. Group, corporate, and direct strategy stay with the hotel's own team; Otamiser manages the leisure and individual OTA business.
Where the Two Differ
OTA ranking tracked as its own daily signal
The Net Revenue's outsourcing service centers on demand forecasting, commercial strategy, and distribution optimization, reported weekly and monthly. Otamiser tracks comp-set rank daily on Booking.com, Expedia, and Agoda as a distinct metric, actively managing Genius, Preferred Partner, and Expedia and Agoda placement rather than leaving them switched on and unoptimized.
One connected operation, not a menu of separate services
The Net Revenue offers audit, outsourcing, consulting, marketing, and commercial representation as distinct services. Otamiser runs pricing and OTA ranking as a single operation on top of the RMS a hotel already uses, with nothing separate to configure or manage internally.

Frequently Asked Questions
We're already talking to The Net Revenue. Why consider Otamiser?
The Net Revenue covers hotel pricing, distribution, and direct-booking strategy well, especially for boutique hotels and chains that want a broader marketing and commercial-representation partner. Otamiser runs pricing and OTA ranking as one operation, on top of the RMS you already use, managing Booking.com, Expedia, and Agoda daily rather than as a periodic review.
Does Otamiser replace our RMS?
No. Otamiser directs the RMS, PMS, and channel manager you already run, adding the OTA ranking, content, and promotion management most revenue tools don't touch.
Will this interfere with our group or corporate strategy?
No. Otamiser manages leisure and individual OTA business. Group, corporate, and direct strategy stay with your team.
What hotel sizes does Otamiser work with?
Independent hotels and groups, from a handful of rooms to around 80.
Is there a minimum contract?
Engagements start with a three-month period.
How fast will we see results?
Most hotels see movement within the first weeks, with revenue following in the same booking window.
