Otamiser Revenue Management vs Rankbreeze
Table of contents
Rankbreeze
and
Otamiser
both help short-term rental listings get seen and booked.
Rankbreeze
is self-serve software built around Airbnb that gives you the data and the rules, and you make the changes.
Otamiser
is a revenue management partner that handles pricing and OTA visibility together, across Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and Expedia, as a team that works inside yours.
Below is what each one does, where they differ and who each one is for.
What Rankbreeze does
Rankbreeze is a software company founded in 2018 that started as an Airbnb rank tracker and now sells to hosts, property managers and revenue managers, with no minimum portfolio size.
Its tools cover Airbnb search rankings (tracked daily across dates and guest counts), A/B testing for titles, photos and prices, booking funnel analytics, and listing tips for photos, amenities, reviews and also offers a done-for-you Listing Optimization Service and an API add-on.
Its tools track Airbnb search positions daily across dates and guest counts, A/B testing for titles, photos and prices, booking funnel analytics, a market scanner for competitor data, and listing tips for photos, amenities and reviews. It also offers a done-for-you Optimized Listing Service, which rewrites your listing content and is paid per property, plus an API add-on.
Since 2022, Rankbreeze has also included a pricing engine in its rules-based plan: prices change only according to the rules you set, and they're sent to a connected PMS (Hospitable, Uplisting and OwnerRez at the time of writing). According to Rankbreeze's own help centre, the engine doesn't react to competitor prices or market changes and doesn't recommend prices.
Rankbreeze focuses on Airbnb and is self-serve: you get the data and make the changes yourself.
What Otamiser does
Otamiser is a revenue management partner for short-term rental property managers and hoteliers. Its revenue managers use AI tools to run pricing, channel setup and visibility across all OTAs like Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and Expedia.
Each client gets a dedicated revenue manager that reviews:
Pricing:
running rates and restrictions every day in PriceLabs, Wheelhouse, Beyond or any other RMS. They sync through your PMS to every channel, including minimum stays and gap nights.
Visibility:
tracking every listing's visibility from day one and turning it into a booking probability. When a listing starts slipping, Otamiser finds out why (cancellation policy, an owner minimum, reviews, price) and acts on it.
OTA setup:
set up and ongoing optimization of each OTA: rate plans, policies, discounts and promotions, including Booking.com programmes like Genius and Preferred Partner.
Your team:
Otamiser works in your Slack or WhatsApp, runs regular performance meetings, and gives your owner-relations team the numbers they need for owner conversations.
Where the two differ
Who does the work
With
Rankbreeze
, you read the data, decide what to change and make the changes.
With
Otamiser
, your dedicated revenue manager does that work and tells you why they did it. When a listing underperforms, you get the reason and what’s being done about it, and it’s shared in your recurring performance meetings.
One booking site or all of them
Rankbreeze
is built around Airbnb.
Otamiser
works on Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and Expedia, because each one decides visibility in its own way. One of the things Otamiser keeps in check is to ensure the channels are balanced with each other, so a change that helps on Airbnb doesn't cost you on Booking.com.
Pricing rules or pricing and visibility together
Rankbreeze's
pricing engine follows the rules you set.
Otamiser
connects pricing to what's happening on the OTAs. Visibility moves before your bookings do: if a listing slips in search, pickup slows a few weeks later. Your revenue manager keeps track of your listing’s visibility so they can act before the drop shows in your reservations.
Not every revenue problem is a pricing problem.
Listing content
Rankbreeze
offers content tips and a paid service that rewrites your listing.
Otamiser
has a different approach to listing optimization as they’ve tested title and description rewrites across more than 100,000 listings and saw no visible impact on visibility. So Otamiser checks your listings against each OTA's guidelines and only changes content when there are any inconsistencies (or something breaks a rule.) They focus on what moves visibility: settings, policies, promotions, price and consistency across the OTAs.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I switch from Rankbreeze to Otamiser?
Yes. Rankbreeze has no lock-in, so you can cancel any time. Otamiser’s revenue managers can easily take over pricing and OTA visibility from there.
Do I need to change my PMS or pricing tool?
No. Otamiser connects to the major PMS’, channel managers and pricing tools.
Will you rewrite my listings?
Only if something breaks an OTA guideline. Rewriting titles and descriptions showed no visible impact across 100,000+ listings, so we focus on what does move visibility.
What's the minimum portfolio size?
We work with professional managers with 20 units or more. Below that, try our free listing health check.
